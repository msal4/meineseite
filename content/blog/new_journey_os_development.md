I realized lately that I have stopped posting blogs for some time. After landing my first job at an ISP company back in my home country of Iraq, I had stopped writing technical articles. My first and last articles: "How to build a news app in react native" and "How to bring a little asynchronous programming to Dart with futures" that date back to around 2018. These articles are 8+ years old, reading them now I can only feel a bit of pride and cringe at the same time. I wrote them when I was ~20 years old. At that time I had no real professional experience in the field. As you can sense the inexperience in the writing and explaining of the topics chosen. So 8 years later, I've decided to delve into a different topic in the programming world... OS Development. A lot has changed since my last articles, and since its unfamiliar territory I would like to document some of it along the way.

I'm coming at this as a working full-stack developer, not a kernel hacker. But I have always admired the work done by OS devs like Bryan Cantrill, Dr. Mckusick, Warner Losh and phk. People who I consider to be legends and want to walk their path. That's why I want to understand the systems I'm using from the ground up and eventually contribute to operating system development.
