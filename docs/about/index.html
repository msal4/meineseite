About

I’m Salman Sarray, a Software Engineer based in Munich, Germany, with over a decade of experience building robust, fast full-stack solutions.

My focus is performance: finding and removing the bottlenecks that slow teams and systems down. That might mean profiling a mobile app’s frame rate, tightening an embedded system’s power budget, right-sizing cloud infrastructure, or rewriting a slow backend query. I work across mobile (Flutter, React Native), embedded and low-level systems, DevOps and cloud architecture, and backend/web development, always with a focus on logic, stability, and speed. Outside of my professional work, I love playing around with various operating systems and constantly trying out new things.

Recent work includes VoiceLine and limehome, where I reduced access failures by 80%, along with an online streaming platform used by millions. Full history on LinkedIn .
